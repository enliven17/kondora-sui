## Connect a wallet

Open Markets and choose Connect wallet. Use a Wallet Standard Sui wallet such as Slush and approve the Sui testnet connection.

## Get test tokens

Your first wallet connection automatically receives 100 test kUSD. No signature or SUI gas is needed for this transfer. In Portfolio, the Daily kUSD faucet offers another 100 kUSD every 24 hours, with a countdown to the next claim. The first gift uses the same cooldown, and reconnecting does not grant another welcome payment.

Get testnet SUI from [the Sui faucet](https://faucet.sui.io/?network=testnet) for deposits and trades. kUSD is a demonstration coin with no monetary value.

## Enable trading

Enter a deposit and choose Enable trading. The first setup creates your shared account and then deposits kUSD in a second wallet approval. Later deposits require one approval. Each account has an on-chain owner check; being shared does not make funds public.

## Open and close

Pick a market, a side, an amount and a leverage level. Review the quote and approve in your wallet. A 1% minimum-share bound protects an open against price movement. See positions in Portfolio and close them while trading is open.

## Claim and withdraw

After the oracle proposal and five-minute dispute window, finalized shares can be claimed into the trading balance. Withdraw an available balance from Portfolio. Positions remain on chain if the browser closes.
